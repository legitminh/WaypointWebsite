Mission control

A little focus goes a long way.

Pick one thing. Give it a little time. Waypoint keeps the next step, the flight, and a copilot in one calm place — no streaks to protect, and no leaderboard.

  1. 01

    Copilot

    A sounding board for the work in front of you. Ask a question, talk through a roadblock, or get ready to focus. Replies can include headings, tables, code, and math. Signed-in Calendar deadlines and Drive notes can join the thread.

  2. 02

    Missions

    Name one objective and choose 15, 25, 45, or 60 minutes. Launch, and a flight path tracks the session while your copilot can speak a gentle check-in. Finish to log the flight — your first one sets a personal best.

  3. 03

    Yours to turn on

    Camera signals and screen sharing start off. Turn them on only if you want a softer check-in or a look at the work on screen. Sessions save events and summaries, not raw video, screenshots, or audio.

How it feels

Sign in. Ask, or launch.

  3. Launch the mission
