The premise

School logistics on one side. The work itself on the other.

Waypoint is for the student who already knows what they sat down to do. Ask handles the question in front of you. Lock-in protects the next block of time. Google Calendar and Drive can join the conversation when you want deadlines and notes nearby — they are never required to start.

  1. 01

    Ask

    Explain it simply, quiz yourself, or make a plan. Answers come back with headings, tables, code, and math. Connect Google when Calendar deadlines or Drive notes belong in the thread.

  2. 02

    Lock in

    Set the minutes and the assignment. Waypoint checks the screen against those goals and nudges you from an always-on-top coach line when you drift.

  3. 03

    A softer landing

    Optional camera checks read stress, heart rate, and breathing so the coach can ease off. If the camera stays off, lock-in still runs.

How it feels

Open it. Ask, or lock in.

  1. Sign in

    Open Waypoint on your computer. No API-key screens, no config files.

  2. Ask

    Send a question, or use the study shortcuts. Link Google only if you want Calendar and Drive in the chat.

  3. Lock in

    Name the work, start the timer, and let the coach watch the screen until you are done.
