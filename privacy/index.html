Privacy statement

Your session, not a recording.

We securely process your data and keep no logs of screen or video recording.

What Waypoint processes

Waypoint asks you to sign in with Google. That sign-in can link your account, Calendar, Drive, and study memory so Copilot and missions can use deadlines, notes, and the work you have already named.

  • Account details from Google sign-in.
  • Calendar and Drive context, when you have linked them.
  • Study memory, chat, and mission summaries you choose to keep.

What we do not keep

We keep no logs of screen or video recording. Camera signals and screen sharing are optional, and both start off. If you turn one on, it is used to coach the mission in the moment.

  • No logs of screen recording.
  • No logs of video recording.
  • No raw video, screenshots, or audio saved from a session.

A session can save events and a summary. That record is not a recording of your screen or camera.

Deleting your data

From the app you can erase study memory, session logs, ship progress, chat history, and the Google-linked account on this computer and in the cloud.
